#### Saved response

The finished product page should scroll through this reusable sequence:

1. A full-screen runway-fashion opening with the product name and price.
2. Clean front and back product-alone views from the active Shopify/POD product.
3. On-body editorial views: front, three-quarter, profile, seated, and back.
4. A close-up of the CARLOPHILLIPS signature embroidery.
5. A large material and construction story based on the best product imagery and current POD material understanding.
6. Short product/model video plus a lightweight motion preview.
7. A customer-controlled 360 presentation generated from the approved product imagery.
8. An interactive, zoomable 3D model when a verified GLB is available; add AR only if the asset and viewer support it.
9. Construction details covering hood, pocket, seams, cuffs, waistband, and branding.
10. Shopify price, sizes, availability, size guide, bag, and checkout.
11. Production, delivery, care, returns, and fulfillment information.

The visual treatment is restrained and premium: oversized runway/product media, dark full-screen panels, sparse typography, minimal jargon, and controlled scrolling inspired by the approved Vollebak/Zara direction without copying their assets or text.

For every subsequent product, repeat the same process: select the POD garment and customization; bind Shopify facts; collect front/back references; generate the media set; curate inaccurate outputs; create 360 and 3D candidates; assemble the Preview; verify desktop/mobile, pricing, options, and checkout; then publish the approved package.

The Signature Hoodie currently has Shopify product views, six curated MODA stills, a flat lay, embroidery/material studies, and still-derived motion. The remaining media additions are a short video, digital 360, and interactive 3D model.
