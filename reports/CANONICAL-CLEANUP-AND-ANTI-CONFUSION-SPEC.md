## 4. Local files and worktrees

No worktree is deleted merely because its branch is old, merged, detached, or absent remotely.

For every worktree, record:

- absolute path;
- branch or detached SHA;
- tracked modifications;
- staged modifications;
- untracked files and total size;
- unpushed commits;
- open PR, if any;
- unique files compared with `origin/main` and the active release branch;
- secret-scan result;
- disposition: `merge`, `archive`, `discard`, or `retain-until-PR-closes`;
- reviewer and approval evidence.

Dirty work must be preserved in a named archive branch or a checksummed external evidence archive before removal. Secrets, caches, dependency folders, generated builds, and credentials must never enter a preservation commit.
